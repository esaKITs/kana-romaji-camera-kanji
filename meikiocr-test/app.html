About, Privacy & Licenses

Designed for beginner Japanese textbooks, including thin print and word spacing. Romaji stays large even for long kana sequences; longer labels may wrap. Recognition is experimental: small furigana, complex backgrounds, and some characters may be missed or misread. Experimental ruby detection requires a separately recognized small kana line above kanji.

Privacy and technology

No generative AI. Text recognition uses Tesseract OCR, a machine-learning-based recognition engine, running in your browser. No cloud AI service is used for image recognition.

Camera images stay on your device. This app does not upload or save camera images, OCR text, or recognition results to a server. Processing is performed locally in browser memory.

No training or improvement use. This app does not collect your images or recognition data to train or improve AI models or the app. No account, advertising, or app analytics is built in.

The OCR software and Japanese language data are downloaded from external hosting services when needed. Standard web hosting and network services may process ordinary connection information, such as IP addresses.

Japanese OCR model credits and license

Japanese OCR uses Tesseract language data (jpn.traineddata) and an optional locally processed Klee One fine-tuned model (klee_jpn.traineddata). The optional model is derived from the Tesseract Japanese model and trained using Klee One fonts (SIL Open Font License 1.1).
