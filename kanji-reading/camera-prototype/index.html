About this preview

For recognition troubleshooting: saves recognized text, boxes and verification results locally. No image is included or uploaded.

あア漢: hiragana, katakana and kanji. ア漢: katakana and kanji. 漢: kanji only.

Label backgrounds are chosen automatically from the image, with multiple stable tones that blend with the original background. Text is dark on light backgrounds and white on dark backgrounds. Use ◐ to cycle original-image visibility through 0%, 25%, 55% and 100%; romaji stays opaque. Portrait and landscape are supported. PP-OCR also uses MeikiOCR to measure potentially clipped text, then verifies it twice with the PP recognizer. Live recognition is experimental. Hold the camera steady. Some readings may be wrong or withheld. Kuromoji is provisional.

Technology & privacy

This tool uses machine-learning OCR on your device. It does not use generative AI, ChatGPT, or a cloud OCR API to process your images.

MeikiOCR Web recognizes Japanese text. ONNX Runtime Web runs its recognition models with WebAssembly. Kuromoji and its bundled IPADIC dictionary currently estimate word readings; this analyzer is provisional. Romaji follows the original camera’s spelling rules, with UniDic-derived pronunciation data and additional reading rules.

Recognition models, code, and dictionaries must be downloaded before processing. Your images, PDFs and recognized text are processed in browser memory and are not uploaded by the app. Mode, long-vowel and original-image visibility preferences are saved on this device.

Readings may be wrong or withheld. This tool does not translate text, generate answers, or train models using your images. Complete offline operation is not guaranteed.

Sudachi is being compared in development and is not the analyzer currently used here. This preview also does not use Tesseract, OpenCV image tracking, or WebGPU.
